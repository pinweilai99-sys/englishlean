## A. Look, Listen and Say（课本 P31）
场景：Mrs Chen 和 Lingling 正在购物（服装店，SALE 50% OFF 打五折）。

1. — **Can I have a new sweater, Mum?**
   — **Yes, dear.**
2. — **Which one do you want, a long one or a short one?**
   — **A short one, please.**
3. — **Which one do you want, the thick one or the thin one?**
   — **The thick one, please.**
4. — **It's nice but a little expensive.**
   — **How about this one?**
5. — **Can I try it on, please?**
   — **Sure.**
6. — **You look good!**
   — **Thanks. I like this one.**

重点短语：a new sweater（一件新毛衣）、try on（试穿）、a little expensive（有点贵）、50% off（打五折）
形容词：nice（好看的）、expensive（贵的）

## B. Listen and Learn（课本 P32）

## C. Let's Practise（课本 P32）

a pair of shoes 一双鞋 / a pair of socks 一双袜子

- — Which one do you want, the long one or the short one?

## F. Let's Read（课本 P34）
> **Zhou Jie has new clothes.** But he doesn't like some of them. The coat is too long. The cap is too big. The scarf is too short. He likes the jeans and the shoes. They're nice.
>
> **Liu Li wears new clothes too.** The coat is nice and thin. The hat is pretty. The skirt is beautiful. She has a new pair of shoes, but she doesn't like them. They're too big.
>
> **Mingming is in a clothes shop.** Which jacket does he want? He wants the green one. He likes the black trousers and the blue shoes. He's happy.

重点短语：new clothes 新衣服、nice and thin 又好看又薄、a new pair of shoes 一双新鞋、in a clothes shop 在服装店里
语法：clothes → clothes（复数）、trouser → trousers（复数）、第三人称单数 wears / likes / wants

**Answer the questions.**
1. Whose cap is too big? → Zhou Jie's cap is too big.
2. Whose coat is nice and thin? → Liu Li's coat is nice and thin.
3. Which jacket does Mingming want, the blue one or the green one? → He wants the green one.
4. What does Mingming like? → He likes the green jacket, the black trousers and the blue shoes.

## 考点归纳（用于复习卷命题）
1. **选择疑问句**：Which one do you want, A or B? — I want the ... one, please.（or 连接两个选项，回答择其一，不用 Yes/No）
2. **购物请求与应答**：Can I have a new sweater, Mum? — Yes, dear. / Can I try it on, please? — Sure. / Can I help you? — Yes, please.
3. **评价与推荐**：It's nice but a little expensive. / How about this one? / You look good! / I like this one.
4. **too + 形容词**：The coat is too long. / The cap is too big. / The scarf is too short.
5. **a pair of + 复数名词**：a pair of shoes / a pair of socks（谓语用单数 is，代词用 they/them 视语境）
6. **名词复数**：sweater→sweaters, jacket→jackets, raincoat→raincoats, scarf→scarves/scarfs, shoe→shoes, sock→socks；clothes / trousers / jeans 恒复数
7. **反义词**：long–short, thick–thin, new–old, big–small, tall–short, hot–cold, slow–fast, happy–sad, old–young, dirty–clean
8. **语音 ar /ɑː/**：farm, card, car, shark, hard, Mark（对比 ir/ur/er 不发 /ɑː/）
9. **一般现在时三单**：lives, has, takes, wears, likes, wants, cleans, feeds, learns；频率副词 often / every day / every morning
10. **跨单元复现**：Can I use/borrow...?（U4）、May I have...? / take good care of（U5）、There is/are、人称代词宾格
