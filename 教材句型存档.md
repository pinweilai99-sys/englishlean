# 教材核心句型存档（Structures，Unit 1–8）

> 来源：课本 Structures 汇总页（P75–77），2026-11-02 拍照录入。
> **用途：本项目的所有产出（练习、测验、故事、游戏、复习材料等）都以这些句型为中心设计。**
> 注：蓝体词只作认读要求（不需会拼写）。

---

## Unit 1 外貌（Appearance）
- **What does he / she look like?**
- He / She **is** tall.
- He / She **has** long hair. / a round face. / big eyes.

## Unit 2 食物与点餐（Food & Ordering）
- **What would you like?**
- **I'd like** a sandwich, / a hamburger, / beef noodles, / some water, **please**.

## Unit 3 电脑能做的事（Computer Skills）
- **I can** search for information / email my friends / send greetings / take online lessons **with the computer**.

## Unit 4 借东西（Borrowing School Supplies）
- **Can I use / borrow** your pencil / glue / brush / dictionary, **please**?
- **Sure. Here you are.**

## Unit 5 请求养宠物（Asking for a Pet）
- **May I have** a pet / a rabbit / a goldfish?
- **Yes, you can. / No, you can't.**

## Unit 6 选择衣物（Choosing Clothes）
- **Which one do you want, the thick one or the thin one?**
- **I want** the thick one. / the thin one. / the short one, please.
- **Can I have** a new sweater, Mum? — Yes, dear.
- **Can I try it on**, please? — Sure.
- **It's nice but a little expensive.** / **How about this one?** / **You look good!**
- **too + 形容词**：The coat is too long. / The cap is too big.
- **a pair of + 复数**：a pair of shoes / a pair of socks
- 反义词：long–short、thick–thin、new–old、big–small、tall–short
- 语音 **ar /ɑː/**：farm、card、car、shark

> **蓝体词只认读不拼写**；替换词优先取本单元词表，避免超纲。

## Unit 7 家务（Chores at Home）
- **What chores do you do at home?**
- **I** make the bed / wash the vegetables / cook a meal / sweep the floor / clean the windows / water the plants.

## Unit 8 爱好（Hobbies）
- **What's your hobby?**
- **My hobby is** taking photos / playing the violin / playing badminton / playing chess / listening to music / reading books.
